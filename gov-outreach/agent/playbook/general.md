# General rules for records requests.

These apply in every place. Code rejects anything here that names a place, agency, law or local system, or that rests on evidence from fewer than two places in two states. Nothing is declared learned before the minimums in `policy.json`.

## Rules

Only `active` rules reach a draft. `proposed` rules wait for a person's spot check. Rules marked `seed` are starting assumptions, with no outcomes behind them yet.
