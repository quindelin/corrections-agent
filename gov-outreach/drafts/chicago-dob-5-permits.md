Subject: FOIA request: plan review correction comments on 5 building permits

Under the Illinois Freedom of Information Act (5 ILCS 140), I request copies of the plan review correction comments the Department of Buildings issued to applicants on the 5 building permits listed below. All 5 are Standard Plan Review permits whose descriptions in the City's Building Permits dataset (ydr8-5enu) mention certified corrections.

## Before you send

- Open the Buildings FOIA page and confirm the address, email and online form. Then record it with confirm-contact.
- Decide the commercial purpose paragraph with a lawyer. Declaring it means 21 working days, lower priority, possible search fees and almost no Public Access Counselor review.
- Paste the whole request into the body of any email or form. The Act doesn't require a public body to open attachments or links.
- Watch for a reply asking you to confirm you're a person. The clock stops, and it can deny after 30 days.
- A search excerpt of the City's FOIA FAQ says requesters' names and request details are posted online. Assume a rival could see this.
- Electronic requests must appear entirely in the body of the submission, and a public body need not open attachments or links (section 3(c)).
- A public body that reasonably believes a request didn't come from a person can require verification. Its clock stops, and it may deny the request after 30 days without a reply (section 3(j)).

## Ask a lawyer

- Is a reviewer's comment an 'other construction related technical document' under section 7(1)(k)? I found no ruling (see docs/chicago-real-data-plan.md).
- Does testing a paid product count as a commercial purpose, and if we declare it, is any Public Access Counselor review left?
- Whether testing a paid product is a 'commercial purpose' under section 2(c-10) is unsettled. Declaring it changes the clock, the fees and the appeal route, including losing most Public Access Counselor review under section 9.5(b). Don't remove the paragraph just to avoid that.
