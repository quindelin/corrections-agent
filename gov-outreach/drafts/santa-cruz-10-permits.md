## The text

```text
Subject: Public records request: building review comments on 10 permits

Hello,

Under the California Public Records Act (Gov. Code § 7920.000 et seq.), I request the building plan review comments on 10 building permits, as a report or export.

**What I am asking for:**

1. For 10 building permits with at least one review marked Correction between January 1 and December 31, 2024, chosen by whatever is easiest for your staff: the comments from each reviewing agency, for each review round.
2. For each comment: the agency, the review round, the determination and the date.

A report or export, as PDF or CSV, is ideal. If the fastest answer is 10 permit numbers whose comments I can read on the Application Status pages, that's fine too. Search clue: these appear in the building application review records behind Application Status.

**What I am not asking for:** plans, drawings, calculations or sealed documents.

**Redactions:** I do not need the name, email, phone number, signature or seal of any private person, so please redact them. A reviewer's discipline is enough in place of the reviewer's name. If you withhold or redact anything else, please release the rest and state the exemption and the factual basis for it.

**Who's asking and why:** [Entity name] builds software that helps architects and permit expediters respond to plan review correction comments, and intends to charge for it. The records would be used to test and improve that software on real comments. [The records will not be resold or republished as a collection. Keep this only if you will keep it.]

**Format and fees:** Please send the records electronically to the email address below. Please tell me before incurring fees above $[amount].

**If this is too much to do at once:** please tell me, and I'd gladly take 3 permits first.

Thank you.

[Your full name]
[Entity name, if any]
[Mailing address]
[Email]
[Phone]
```

## Before you send

- Using the portal may mean accepting its terms. That's your decision, so read them first.
- Use the County's designated method. Under AB 1821 as read, other routes may not start the deadline from January 1, 2027.
- Requests received in 2026 use 10 calendar days. From 2027, 10 business days as read. Check the chaptered text.
- The permission ask promises to remove names and not to republish without asking. Send it only if you'll keep that.
- Under AB 1821 as read, a request counts as properly made only through a method the agency designates. Use the listed method so the deadline applies. Unconfirmed against the chaptered text.

## Ask a lawyer

- Read the chaptered text of AB 1821 for what it changes, and whether commercial use affects fees.
- Read the chaptered text of AB 1821 (Chapter 899, Statutes of 2026) on fees, submission methods and its start date. Ask whether it changes how a commercial request is treated.
