## The text

```text
Subject: Public records request: plan review comments on 10 building permits

Hello,

Under the Arizona Public Records Law (A.R.S. § 39-121 et seq.), I request the plan review comments on 10 recent commercial building permits, as a report or export.

**What I am asking for:**

1. For 10 commercial building permits (TC-COM numbers) that received a Requires Resubmit status on a review completed between January 1 and March 31, 2026, chosen by whatever is easiest for your staff: the review comments for each discipline and each review version.
2. For each comment: the review type, the status, the version number and the review date.

A report or export, as PDF or CSV, is ideal. If the fastest answer is 10 permit numbers whose comments I can read myself on Property Research Online, that's fine too.

**What I am not asking for:** plans, drawings, calculations or sealed documents.

**Redactions:** I do not need the name, email, phone number, signature or seal of any private person, so please redact them. A reviewer's discipline is enough in place of the reviewer's name. If you withhold or redact anything else, please release the rest and state the exemption and the factual basis for it.

**Statement of commercial purpose (A.R.S. § 39-121.03(A)):** These records are for a commercial purpose. [Entity name] builds software that helps architects and permit expediters respond to plan review correction comments, and intends to charge for it. The records would be used to test and improve that software on real comments. [The records will not be resold or republished as a collection. Keep this only if you will keep it.]

**Format and fees:** Please send the records electronically to the email address below. Please tell me before incurring fees above $[amount].

**If this is too much to do at once:** please tell me, and I'd gladly take 3 permits first.

Thank you.

[Your full name]
[Entity name, if any]
[Mailing address]
[Email]
[Phone]
```

## Before you send

- Open the Clerk's Public Records page. Confirm the email and whether the City requires its form. If so, copy this text into it.
- Tick commercial on the form and complete the commercial fee form. Ask for the amount first, because Arizona allows a charge for commercial value.
- Read the PRO disclaimer yourself before sending the permission ask.
- Decide whether the records request goes to the Clerk or to TDC Online.
- The permission ask promises to remove names and not to republish without asking. Send it only if you'll keep that.

## Ask a lawyer

- Check the wording of the commercial purpose statement, and how the fee rule in A.R.S. 39-121.03 would apply.
- Does 'for personal use' on the PRO disclaimer limit a company's testing use?
- Have a lawyer check the statement's wording. A commercial requester may pay a share of the cost of obtaining the record, the labor cost of reproducing it, and its commercial value as determined by the public body (39-121.03(A)). Misusing a record or misstating the purpose can bring damages of three times the fee (39-121.03(C)). Also ask whether a City form must be used for the statement.
