```text
Subject: Public records request: 5 building plan review correction letters

Hello,

Under the Washington Public Records Act (chapter 42.56 RCW), I request 5 building plan review correction letters the City issued between January 1 and June 30, 2026, chosen by whatever is easiest for your staff.

**What I am asking for:**

1. The correction letter or list of corrections sent to the applicant, as issued, for each of the 5 permits.
2. For each: the permit number and the date issued.

PDF is ideal.

**What I am not asking for:** plans, drawings, calculations or sealed documents.

**Redactions:** I do not need the name, email, phone number, signature or seal of any private person, so please redact them. A reviewer's discipline is enough in place of the reviewer's name. If you withhold or redact anything else, please release the rest and state the exemption and the factual basis for it.

**Who's asking and why:** [Entity name] builds software that helps architects and permit expediters respond to plan review correction comments, and intends to charge for it. The records would be used to test and improve that software on real comments. [The records will not be resold or republished as a collection. Keep this only if you will keep it.] This isn't a request for a list of individuals, and I'd rather have names removed.

**Format and fees:** Please send the records electronically to the email address below. Please tell me before incurring fees above $[amount].

**If this is too much to do at once:** please tell me, and I'd gladly take 2 letters first.

Thank you.

[Your full name]
```

## Before you send

- Open the Public Records page, confirm the portal, then record it with confirm-contact. Read the portal's terms first.
- File with the general portal, not the separate police portal.
- Requests must be for identifiable records (RCW 42.56.080(1)). Name the record type and the date range.
- An agency may deny a request it reasonably believes a program or script generated, if it is one of several from the requester within 24 hours and they cause excessive interference (RCW 42.56.080(3)). A person sends these, one per agency.

## Ask a lawyer

- Do any conditions apply to using these commercially?
- Ask a lawyer whether comment records that name people could be treated as a list of individuals. We state the purpose anyway, because hiding it is not worth the risk and the agency may ask.
