# Chicago rules

**Revision marks.** Under Certified Plan Corrections, a correction that revises drawings says the revision will be bubbled, dated and initialed. This includes clerical sheet fixes. It doesn't apply to zoning items, or to items with no drawing change, such as owner paperwork or another agency's approval.
